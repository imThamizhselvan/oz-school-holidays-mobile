Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 March 2026

AU School Holidays ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application AU School Holidays (the "App") handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the App's features:

  • Device Location (optional): With your explicit permission, we access your approximate or precise device location solely to automatically detect which Australian state you are in, so we can display the relevant school holiday dates. We do not store, transmit, or share your location with any third party.
  • Notification Preferences: If you enable push notifications, your notification settings (e.g. how many days before a holiday to notify you) are stored locally on your device only using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Selected State & Year: Your chosen state and year preference are saved locally on your device to remember your settings between sessions.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

3. Location Permission

The App requests the Location When In Use permission only to auto-detect your Australian state. You may deny this permission and manually select your state instead. Location data is used in real-time and is never stored or sent anywhere.

4. Push Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App schedules local notifications on your device to remind you of upcoming school holidays. These are local notifications only — no data is sent to any server. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or within the App's Settings tab.

5. Data Storage

All app data (state selection, year preference, notification settings) is stored entirely on your device using standard local storage. We have no access to this data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. No personal data is collected at all.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
